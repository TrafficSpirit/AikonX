Questions, answered

Which email platforms do you work with?

We work with the major platforms, including HubSpot, Klaviyo, Mailchimp and ActiveCampaign, and can recommend one if you do not have a platform yet.

Is email marketing compliant with spam laws?

Yes. We follow Australia's Spam Act requirements, including consent, clear sender identification and easy unsubscribe options.

How often should we email our list?

It depends on your audience and content. Automated sequences run on triggers, and regular newsletters typically work well every two to four weeks.
