Facebook ads cost: the Australian benchmarks

In Australia, Facebook ads typically cost AUD $1.15–$3.20 per click and AUD $11–$15 per 1,000 impressions. For most industries, a lead costs AUD $20–$50. Instagram placements run through the same Meta Ads platform and usually cost a little more: around AUD $1.50–$4.00 per click and AUD $12–$18 per 1,000 impressions.

These are ranges, not quotes. What you actually pay depends on who you target, what you ask them to do and how good your creative is. The factors on the right move your costs the most.

What drives your cost

  • Campaign objective: traffic is cheaper than conversions
  • Audience size and competition for it
  • Creative quality: more relevant ads cost less
  • Time of year: costs spike in November–December
  • Bidding strategy
  • Industry: finance and legal audiences cost more per click

Facebook ads cost: questions, answered

How much does Facebook advertising cost in Australia?

In Australia, Facebook ads typically cost AUD $1.15–$3.20 per click (CPC) and AUD $11–$15 per 1,000 impressions (CPM) in 2026. Cost per lead ranges from AUD $20–$50 for most industries. These figures vary by industry, audience size, creative quality and campaign objective.

How much should I budget for Facebook ads in Australia?

A minimum test budget of AUD $1,500–$3,000 per month in ad spend is recommended to generate meaningful data. For lead-generation campaigns, AUD $3,000–$6,000/month is a common starting point. E-commerce brands scaling profitably typically invest AUD $5,000–$20,000+/month.

How much does a social media agency charge to manage Facebook ads in Australia?

Australian agencies typically charge AUD $800–$2,500/month for management fees at smaller spend levels, rising to 10–15% of ad spend for larger budgets. AIkonX management fees start from AUD $1,200/month, with no percentage-of-spend mark-up on your ad budget.

Is $10 a day enough for Facebook ads?

AUD $10/day ($300/month) can generate impressions and limited clicks, but it is rarely enough to generate reliable lead or sales data. Meta's algorithm needs approximately 50 conversions per ad set per week to exit the learning phase, which typically requires a higher daily budget — usually AUD $30–$100/day depending on your cost per conversion.

How much do Instagram ads cost in Australia?

Instagram ads run through the same Meta Ads platform as Facebook. CPCs for Instagram placements in Australia average AUD $1.50–$4.00, and CPMs average AUD $12–$18, slightly higher than Facebook placements due to higher engagement rates.

What affects the cost of Facebook ads?

Key factors include your campaign objective (traffic is cheaper than conversions), audience size and competition, creative quality (higher relevance scores lower costs), time of year (costs spike in November–December), and your bidding strategy. Narrow or high-value audiences such as finance or legal typically cost more per click.
