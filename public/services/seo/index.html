SEO services FAQs

How much do SEO services cost in Australia?

It depends on your market, your competition and how much work your site needs. Every AIkonX proposal sets out the monthly price and exactly what is included before you commit. Ask for a free SEO review and we will quote against your goals.

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes can show results within weeks. Meaningful growth in rankings and enquiries usually builds over three to six months, depending on your competition and starting point.

Is SEO worth it for a small business?

Usually, yes, if you serve a clear area and your customers search before they buy. SEO keeps bringing in enquiries after the work is done, unlike ads that stop when you stop paying. If SEO is not the right first step for you, we will tell you.

What do SEO services include?

Keyword research, technical SEO, on-page optimisation, content, link building, local SEO and reporting. The mix depends on what your site needs most.

How do I choose an SEO company in Melbourne?

Look for transparent pricing and deliverables, real case studies, a named team and reporting tied to leads rather than just rankings. Be wary of anyone who guarantees first-page rankings, because no one controls Google.

Is there a minimum contract?

SEO builds over months, so we plan in 90-day cycles and review results with you every month. Your proposal spells out the contract terms before you sign anything.

Do you use AI to write content?

We use AI for research and analysis, but every page is written, fact-checked and edited by people.
