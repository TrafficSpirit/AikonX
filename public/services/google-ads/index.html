Questions, answered

How much should we spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your market and goals. We work best with accounts investing at least a few thousand dollars a month, and we will recommend a starting budget based on search volume and your cost per lead.

Do we keep ownership of the ad account?

Always. The account, data and assets are yours. We work in it with manager access.

How quickly will we see results?

Paid search can produce leads within days of launch. Performance typically improves over the first 60 to 90 days as we gather data and refine targeting.
