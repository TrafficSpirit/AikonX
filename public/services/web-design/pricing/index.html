What is the average website design cost in Australia?

The average cost for a professionally designed business website in Australia is $7,000–$10,000 AUD, including design, development, SEO foundations and a content management system. Template-based builds are cheaper but rarely match the performance of a custom site.

What affects web design cost in Australia?

Key factors include number of pages, custom vs template design, CMS requirements (WordPress, headless, custom), e-commerce functionality, integrations (CRM, booking, payment), copywriting, photography, SEO work and ongoing hosting or maintenance.

Website cost FAQs

How much does a website cost in Australia?

A professionally built business website in Australia typically costs between $2,500 and $25,000+ AUD. A basic 5–8 page site runs $2,500–$5,000, a mid-range business site with custom design and CMS costs $6,000–$12,000, and complex or enterprise builds start from $15,000.

What is the average website design cost in Australia?

The average cost for a professionally designed business website in Australia is $7,000–$10,000 AUD, including design, development, SEO foundations and a content management system. Template-based builds are cheaper but rarely match the performance of a custom site.

What affects web design cost in Australia?

Key factors include number of pages, custom vs template design, CMS requirements (WordPress, headless, custom), e-commerce functionality, integrations (CRM, booking, payment), copywriting, photography, SEO work and ongoing hosting or maintenance.

Is a cheap website worth it?

A $500–$1,500 DIY or template site can work for brand-new businesses testing an idea, but for established businesses competing for leads online, a poorly built site typically costs more in lost conversions than the saving on build fees. Speed, SEO structure and user experience all directly affect enquiry rates.

Do you charge ongoing fees after the website launches?

Hosting, domain and SSL are ongoing costs regardless of agency. AIkonX offers optional care plans from $150/month covering security updates, backups, uptime monitoring and minor content changes. These are separate from any ongoing SEO or ads retainer.

How long does it take to build a website?

A starter site can launch in 3–4 weeks if content is ready. A full business website typically takes 6–10 weeks from kick-off to launch, including design reviews and content entry. Complex builds with integrations or e-commerce take 10–16 weeks.
