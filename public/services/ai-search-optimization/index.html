Search has moved beyond ten blue links

AI assistants answer questions directly, often naming a handful of brands and skipping the click entirely. If your business is not part of those answers, you are invisible to a growing share of buyers, even if you rank well in traditional search.

Generative engine optimization (GEO) shapes how AI models understand and describe your brand. We structure your content so it is easy for models to quote, strengthen the signals they use to judge credibility, and track how often you appear in AI answers compared with your competitors.

Questions, answered

Is GEO different from SEO?

They overlap. Strong SEO foundations help, but AI engines also weigh clarity, consistency and third-party mentions differently. GEO focuses on those extra signals.

Which AI platforms do you cover?

We track ChatGPT, Google Gemini and AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot and Perplexity, and adjust as new platforms gain users.

Can you control what AI says about us?

Not directly. We influence it by improving the information models draw on, and we flag inaccurate answers so they can be corrected at the source.
