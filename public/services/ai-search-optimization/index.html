AI search optimisation for Australian businesses

AI search optimisation is the work of making your business easy for AI search tools to understand, check and describe accurately. AIkonX is a Melbourne digital marketing agency. We benchmark how AI tools talk about you today, fix the content and data behind those answers, and re-test the same way each month. We can't promise any AI platform will mention you. We can show you what it says now and what changed.

Search has moved beyond ten blue links

People now ask AI assistants a question and get one written answer that names a handful of businesses. Some never click a result. AI answers are one more place buyers may meet your brand, and what appears varies by platform, question and person.

That is why AI SEO sits beside conventional organic search rather than replacing it. We assess both. Your site still needs to be fast, clear and credible for Google. AI search optimisation adds a focus on how your brand and your pages show up inside generated answers.

Who it's for

  • Brands in competitive, research-heavy categories, where buyers compare options before they enquire.
  • Businesses whose organic clicks have flattened.
  • B2B companies with long buying cycles, where a shortlist is formed early.
  • Service businesses whose customers search by suburb and ask AI tools for recommendations.
  • Teams that want a measured baseline before they spend more.

If you want a guaranteed placement in ChatGPT, Gemini or Google AI Overviews, we aren't the right fit. Nobody can honestly sell that.

What our AI search optimisation service includes

We tailor the work to your customers, service area and category. One national prompt set doesn't represent every market.

AI visibility benchmark

We test a defined set of buyer questions across the AI experiences agreed for your engagement. We record brand mentions, cited sources and which competitors appear. Results can vary by prompt, location, account and time, so we note the conditions of every check. If you want to know why two reports disagree, read why AI visibility reports can differ.

Content and SEO improvements

We find the pages that need a clearer answer, useful evidence and better structure. Then we recommend or prepare the updates. Where the fix is a new guide or a rewritten service page, our content marketing team can write it. Where it is technical, it sits with our SEO service.

Structured data and entity consistency

We review the schema on your key pages and check that your business name, services, address and phone number match across your website, Google Business Profile and directories. Markup helps describe a page. It does not guarantee rankings or AI citations.

Measurement and review

We document the prompt set, the platforms checked, what we saw and what to do next. That lets every later review be compared on the same basis.

Generative engine optimisation (GEO)

Generative engine optimisation, or GEO, is the research-world name for much of this work. You may also see it written as generative engine optimization, or as AI search optimization in US spelling. The terms overlap with answer engine optimisation. We treat them as one job: clear answers, checkable evidence and consistent business details.

For the definition, where the term came from and how it differs from SEO, read our plain-English guide: what is generative engine optimisation (GEO)?

Questions, answered

Can you guarantee that an AI platform will mention my business?

No. AI answers change and may differ by query, location, user context and platform. We can assess observed visibility and improve the clarity and consistency of information available about your business, but cannot control whether a platform cites or recommends it.

Is AI search optimisation different from SEO?

It overlaps with SEO. Technical accessibility, useful content and credible information remain relevant; GEO adds a focus on how your brand and sources appear in selected AI-generated answers. It is not a substitute for a sound SEO foundation.

Which platforms do you measure?

We agree the platforms and prompt set for the work, then record exactly what was checked. Availability and reporting features differ between platforms.

Can you control what AI says about us?

Not directly. We influence it by improving the information models draw on, and we flag inaccurate answers so they can be corrected at the source.

What do your AI SEO services include?

An AI visibility benchmark, content and SEO improvements, a structured data and business information review, and ongoing measurement. We document the prompts, platforms and conditions so each review can be compared with the last.

Do you work with businesses outside Melbourne?

Yes. We are based in St Kilda East, Melbourne, and work with businesses across Australia. We build the prompt set around your own customers and service area.
