Call tracking shows you which ads, keywords and web pages make your phone ring, so you can stop guessing where your best leads come from. Phone calls are your missing data

For many businesses, the best leads call rather than fill in a form. Without call tracking, those calls are invisible in your analytics. Channels that drive phone enquiries then look like they are underperforming, and they get their budgets cut.

With call tracking, each call is tied to the campaign, keyword or page that produced it. You see which marketing earns calls, and ad platforms can optimise toward them.

Who it's for

  • Service businesses that win work by phone
  • Multi-location businesses with several numbers
  • Advertisers running Google Ads
  • Teams who want to review call quality and handling

How call tracking works for an Australian business

Each marketing source gets its own number. Calls forward to the line you already answer, and every call is logged against its source. Your main number stays on your Google Business Profile and directory listings, while tracking numbers do their work on your website, in ads and on printed material.

That is the short version. Our guide, call tracking explained for Australian businesses, covers the mechanics and the questions owners ask most.

Call recording and caller notifications

Recording is optional. We switch it on only if you choose it, and when you do, we set up the caller notifications. Recordings can capture personal information, so decide who can listen, how long you keep them, and what your privacy policy says about them.

Rules on recording calls and notifying callers are a legal matter, and we do not give legal advice. Before you record, confirm your obligations with a lawyer or the relevant regulator. Our explainer covers where to start.

Call tracking questions, answered

Will customers notice different numbers?

No. Calls forward to your existing line, and the number a visitor sees changes automatically based on how they arrived.

Does call tracking hurt local SEO?

Not when it is set up correctly. We keep your main number consistent on your Google Business Profile and directory listings, and use tracking numbers on your website and in ads.

Are calls recorded?

Only if you choose to enable recording. When you do, we set up the caller notifications. Rules on recording differ by situation, so check your obligations with a lawyer or the relevant regulator.

Can call tracking send calls to Google Ads and GA4?

Yes. We send calls to Google Ads and GA4 as conversions, so bidding and reports reflect phone enquiries as well as clicks.

Can you track calls across several locations?

Yes. Multi-location businesses can have separate numbers for each location, and calls are reported by channel, campaign and keyword. Tell us which locations you run and we will recommend a structure.

How much does call tracking software cost in Australia?

It depends on the number of tracked sources and locations, whether you record calls, and how much integration and reporting you need. We recommend a setup after we learn how customers reach you, then give you a price for it.

Is call tracking worth it for a small business?

If phone calls bring in most of your work, usually yes. If few calls come from marketing, we will say so before you commit.
