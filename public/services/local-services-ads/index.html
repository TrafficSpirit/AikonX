Questions, answered

Is my business eligible?

Eligibility depends on your service category and location, which Google expands over time. We check this first, before you commit to anything.

How is this different from Google Ads?

Local Services Ads charge per lead and show your reviews and verification badge, while regular Google Ads charge per click. Many service businesses run both.

How long does verification take?

It varies by category and how quickly documents are provided, often anywhere from a few days to a few weeks.
