Questions, answered

How much traffic do we need for remarketing?

Google needs a minimum audience size to serve ads, usually at least a few hundred active users over the past month. We will check your volumes before recommending it.

Is remarketing privacy compliant?

Yes, when set up properly. We use platform-approved tools and work with your privacy policy and cookie consent setup.

Will people find the ads annoying?

Not if they are managed well. We cap how often ads appear and stop showing them once someone converts.
