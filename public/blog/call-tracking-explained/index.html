Call Tracking Explained: How It Works

Call tracking shows you which marketing makes your phone ring. It works by giving each source of calls, such as a Google ad, your website or a flyer, its own phone number. Every call is logged against that source and forwarded to your normal line. The recording rules in Australia need care, so they are covered below.

By Anisa Chong

For many businesses, the best leads call rather than fill in a form. Without call tracking, those calls show up nowhere in your analytics, so a campaign that makes the phone ring can look like it is failing.

What is call tracking?

Call tracking is software that attributes phone calls to the marketing that caused them. A website form comes with a built-in record of where the visitor came from. A phone call does not. Call tracking fills that gap.

A typical setup records the date and time of each call, the number that was dialled, and the campaign, keyword or page that number belongs to. Some tools add call recordings and summaries. The result is a report of calls by channel, campaign and keyword, which you can read next to your other leads.

What is dynamic number insertion?

Dynamic number insertion (often shortened to DNI) is how call tracking works on a website. A small script on your site swaps the phone number a visitor sees, based on how they arrived.

Someone who clicks your Google ad sees one number. Someone who finds you through the map listing or a search result sees another. A visitor who types your address directly sees a third. Each number forwards to your existing line, so you answer calls the way you always have. Customers do not notice the difference. AIkonX describes its own setup this way on its call tracking service page: unique numbers are shown to visitors from each source, and numbers change automatically based on how each visitor arrived.

Two related tools sit alongside it:

  • Campaign numbers. A fixed number for print, radio, signage or a vehicle wrap, where there is no website to swap anything.
  • Ad platform integration. Calls can be sent to Google Ads and GA4 as conversions, so ad bidding can learn from real phone enquiries instead of clicks alone.

Does call tracking hurt local SEO?

It does not have to. Your Google Business Profile and directory listings should keep one consistent main number, and the tracking numbers should be used on the website and in ads. When it is set up that way, your listings stay consistent. If you use call tracking, ask whoever sets it up how they will protect that main number.

Can you record calls in Australia?

Many call tracking tools can record calls, and recording is usually optional. Whether you may record, and what you must say, depends on where the people on the call are.

The rules are general information here, not legal advice. Sprintlaw, an Australian law firm, says in an article updated on 19 November 2026 that call recording is mainly regulated by state and territory surveillance laws, and that the rules differ. It says Queensland, Victoria and the Northern Territory generally allow a person who is part of a private conversation to record it, subject to limits on later use and sharing. It says New South Wales, the ACT, South Australia, Western Australia and Tasmania generally require the consent of all parties, with narrow exceptions.

Because a business can take calls from anywhere, Sprintlaw's advice is to treat all-party consent as the default. That means telling callers before recording starts. It also says that:

  • Notice should come early, before recording begins, and say why you record.
  • Callers should have a real way to continue without being recorded where possible.
  • Recordings and transcripts can contain personal information, so privacy obligations such as the Australian Privacy Principles may apply. Sprintlaw says the OAIC's APP 5 guidance recognises that a collection notice can be given at the start of a call, using a script or automated message.
  • You should limit who can listen, decide how long you keep recordings, and say in your privacy policy that you record calls.

AIkonX's approach is to make recording optional and to set up the required caller notifications when a client chooses it. If you plan to record, check the rules that apply to your callers and business with a lawyer or the relevant regulator.

What does call tracking mean for a tradie?

Trade searches often come from a person with a problem to fix, on a phone and in a hurry, so calls are the main lead for many trades. Our marketing for tradies page covers the full picture. Three common uses:

  • Judging ads fairly. Our guide to Google Ads for tradies explains why counting clicks instead of jobs misleads you. Call tracking tells you which keyword made the phone ring.
  • Comparing marketplaces with your own sources. Give each source its own number, then work out the cost per booked job. We walk through that in our guide to hipages alternatives for tradies.
  • Spotting missed calls. A call that rang out is a wasted click or a wasted lead. A log of calls shows when they happen.

From call tracking to attribution

A call count is useful. It becomes more useful next to your forms, ad spend and sales. That is the job of attribution: connecting ad platforms, analytics, call tracking and a CRM into one view, so you can see which channels lead to jobs and revenue. Our attribution and reporting service does this with a live dashboard and a plain-English monthly review. No attribution model is perfect, especially with privacy changes. The aim is a view accurate enough to make confident budget decisions.

What should you do next?

Write down every place your phone number appears today: your website, your Google Business Profile, ads, vehicles, flyers and directories. Then decide which of those you want to measure. Start with the biggest spend.

Frequently asked questions

How does call tracking work?

Each marketing source gets its own phone number. When someone calls that number, the call is logged against the source and forwarded to your existing line. On a website, dynamic number insertion changes the number shown based on how the visitor arrived.

Is it legal to record calls in Australia?

It can be, but the rules differ by state and territory. An Australian law firm advises businesses that take calls from several states to tell callers before recording and to treat all-party consent as the default. This is general information, not legal advice.

Will customers notice a different phone number?

No. Calls forward to your existing line, and the number a visitor sees changes automatically based on how they arrived.

Does call tracking affect my Google Business Profile?

Not when it is set up correctly. Keep one consistent main number on your Google Business Profile and directory listings, and use tracking numbers on your website and ads.
