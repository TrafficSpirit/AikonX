Google says it is scaling ads in AI Mode in Australia. Here is what to check in your account

Yes, Google has named Australia. In a post dated 28 October 2026, Google said it is "scaling ads in AI Mode" in Australia and nine other markets. PPC Land, reporting on 1 November 2026, says Google's help page lists Australia for ads in both AI Overviews and AI Mode, that existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible, and that advertisers cannot opt out of these placements separately from Search. Google's post gives no Australian start date and does not mention separate reporting. Below: who said what, what is still unknown, and a checklist for your account.

By AIkonX13 November 2026

What Google has said

  • 28 October 2026, Google Ads & Commerce Blog. In "Ads in AI Mode expand to new markets", Shashi Thakur, VP and GM of Google Search Ads and Ads on Google Experiences, wrote: "Now we're scaling ads in AI Mode in Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines, and Singapore." The post adds that Google plans to expand in Q4 2026 to more locales, including Brazil, Mexico and the United Kingdom.
  • Labelling. The same post says ads "will be clearly labeled as 'Sponsored'".
  • Google's figures. The post says queries in AI Mode are on average three times longer than traditional searches, and that click-through rates are "already similar" between sponsored and organic product list items in AI Mode. Its footnotes cite the Google AI Mode Report (US, May 2026) and Google internal data (US, 20 August to 3 September 2026).
  • Google Ads Help page, as described by PPC Land on 1 November 2026. PPC Land says the page, as of 1 November, lists Australia among the countries where ads can appear within AI Overviews and AI Mode. English is the only language listed. PPC Land adds that Pakistan is marked as text ads only and Indonesia as AI Overviews only.
  • Eligibility, per PPC Land. Advertisers do not need new campaigns or separate budgets. Existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible through the regular Google Ads auction. Text ads and Shopping ads can appear in both places.
  • Keyword matching, per PPC Land. AI Max for Search, broad match, Shopping and Performance Max are eligible for ads matched to longer, exploratory questions. Exact and phrase match keywords are eligible only when the search itself shows clear commercial intent.
  • Controls, per PPC Land. Negative keywords, asset pinning and brand exclusions continue to apply. Advertisers cannot target AI Overviews or AI Mode on their own, or opt out of them separately from Search. PPC Land also says Google can place "AI explainers" around ads, and that advertisers cannot write, edit, approve or opt out of them.
  • Reporting and billing, per PPC Land. Impressions, clicks, conversions and cost from these placements are folded into standard campaign reports, and the queries that trigger them appear in the search terms report in aggregate. PPC Land says the help page describes no segment that separates AI Mode from the rest of Search, and that billing follows the standard Search model with no extra charge for the placement.

What is not confirmed for Australia

  • A start date or rollout level. Google's post says it is "scaling" ads in AI Mode in Australia. It gives no date, no share of searches and no share of advertisers.
  • Performance. Both figures in Google's post come from US data. The click-through comparison covers product list items only, and the post publishes no rates. PPC Land notes that the comparison says nothing about text ads or about the ten new markets.
  • Sensitive categories. PPC Land reports that Google's post does not say whether the exclusions it describes for AI Overviews ads (adult, alcohol, gambling, finance, healthcare and politics) apply to AI Mode in the new markets. If you advertise in finance, health or a similar area, treat this as unknown.
  • Separate reporting. Google's post does not mention a report for AI Mode ads, and PPC Land says the help page describes none.
  • Follow-up questions. Google's post says it is launching multiturn ads, which show on follow-up questions, in the US. It names no other country or date.
  • What your customers see. Google's post gives no Australian screenshots or layouts. Layouts in AI features change often. For one recent example, see our post on brand AI Overviews moving to Google's right column.

What to check in your account this week

  1. Campaign types and eligibility. List your active Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns. Note which use broad match, exact or phrase match, or AI Max, because PPC Land reports that matching rules differ by type.
  2. Landing pages. Open the page each ad sends people to. Check that prices, services, service areas and opening hours are correct and match the ad.
  3. Ad copy and assets. Read your headlines, descriptions and images as a stranger would. Remove old offers, retired services and wrong suburbs.
  4. Conversion tracking. Confirm that form fills, calls and sales are counted, and that the conversion actions you bid on are the ones that matter to the business. If results from these placements sit inside normal reports, clean tracking is how you judge whether the ads pay.
  5. Search terms report. Review it weekly. Look for longer, more conversational phrasing you have not seen before, and add negative keywords where the intent is wrong.
  6. Brand and keyword settings. Check your negative keyword lists and brand exclusions, which PPC Land reports still apply. Decide whether broad match and AI Max suit your account.
  7. Reporting. Look in your account for any AI Mode or AI Overviews segment. If you cannot find one, treat any separate AI Mode figure quoted to you with caution until its source is shown.
  8. Keep dated notes. Each week, record the date, impressions, clicks, conversions and cost per conversion by campaign, any changes you made, and anything unusual in the search terms. If results shift, you have a record to compare against.

FAQ

Has Google confirmed ads in AI Mode for Australia?

In part. Google's post of 28 October 2026 names Australia among the markets where it is scaling ads in AI Mode. It does not give an Australian start date.

Do I need new campaigns?

PPC Land reports, citing Google's help page, that you do not. Existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible, with no separate budget.

Can I opt out?

PPC Land reported on 1 November 2026 that advertisers cannot opt out of AI Mode or AI Overviews separately from Search. Negative keywords, asset pinning and brand exclusions still apply.

Will AI Mode results show in their own report?

PPC Land says the help page describes no separate segment. Results are included in standard campaign reports, and triggering queries appear in the search terms report in aggregate.

Should I change my budget because of this?

Google's post does not tell advertisers to change budgets. Check your tracking and ad accuracy first, then judge any change on your own conversion data.
