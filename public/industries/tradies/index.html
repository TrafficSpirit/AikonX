Digital marketing for tradies: what we run for trade businesses

Trade businesses win work from people who are searching now, often on a phone and in a hurry. Searches like "emergency plumber near me", "licensed electrician Melbourne" or "concreter quotes" come from people with a problem to fix. Digital marketing for tradies is usually a mix of the five areas below. Each one links to a service page or guide with more detail.

What your customers search for

  • "Emergency plumber near me"
  • "Electrician [suburb]"
  • "Bathroom renovation builder"
  • "Concreter quotes"
  • "Roof repair [suburb]"

SEO for your website

SEO makes your website easy for Google to match to what people search for. For a trade business that means one page for each main service, pages for the suburbs you actually travel to, your licence and insurance shown clearly, and photos of your own jobs. It builds slowly, but the pages keep working when you stop paying for anything. Our guide covers the basics step by step, and our SEO service covers the full programme if you would rather hand it over.

Local SEO and your Google Business Profile

Most trade searches are local, so the map results matter. Local SEO covers your Google Business Profile, consistent business details across directories, a steady process for asking customers for reviews, and location pages that say something real about each area. Google has also changed how suggested edits and promotions work in listings, so a regular check of your profile is worth the time. Our short checklist covers what was reported.

Google Ads

Search ads can put your business in front of someone searching for a service today, while organic work builds. They stop the moment you stop paying, so many trades run ads and SEO together. What you spend depends on your suburb, your trade and what competitors bid. The method we use is to work backwards from what a job is worth to you, cut wasted clicks with negative keywords and send each service to its own page. Our guide shows the arithmetic with made-up figures.

Call tracking

For most trades the best leads ring rather than fill in a form. Without call tracking, those calls are invisible in your analytics, so a campaign that makes the phone ring can look like it is failing. We set up unique numbers for each source and forward every call to your existing line. Calls are sent to Google Ads and GA4 as conversions, and your main number stays consistent on your Google Business Profile. Call recording is optional, with the required caller notifications.

Alternatives to lead marketplaces

If you buy leads from marketplaces, it helps to have a second source of work you control. Your website, Google Business Profile, reviews and search ads build visibility under your own name, and the pages and profile keep working when you pause spend. If you'd rather pay per lead than per click, we also set up and manage Google Local Services Ads for trade businesses that qualify. Eligibility depends on your trade and location, so we check that first. We can run these alongside any marketplace you already use and track calls, so you can compare the cost per job from each source.
