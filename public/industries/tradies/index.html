Questions, answered

How much does SEO cost for a tradie?

For most sole traders and small trade businesses in Australia, local SEO starts at around $800 to $1,500 per month. A growing trade business targeting several suburbs or service types typically invests $1,500 to $3,000 per month. Every AIkonX proposal sets out the price and exactly what is included before you commit. Ask for a free SEO review and we will quote against your goals.

How long before SEO works for a plumber or tradie?

Google Business Profile and citation fixes often produce map pack movement within 4 to 8 weeks. Organic rankings for competitive service terms typically build over 3 to 6 months. We report on progress every month so you can see exactly what has moved and why.

Do I need SEO if I already run Google Ads?

Yes, and the two work well together. Ads stop the moment you stop paying. SEO builds an asset that keeps generating calls and quote requests month after month at a falling cost per lead. Many tradies run both: ads for immediate pipeline, SEO for long-term growth.

What trades do you work with?

We work with plumbers, electricians, builders, concreters, painters, landscapers, roofers, HVAC technicians and other trade businesses across Melbourne and Australia. The same local SEO fundamentals apply across trades, with keyword and content work tailored to each.

Is SEO worth it for a one-person trade business?

Often yes, particularly for high-value jobs like plumbing, electrical and building work where a single new customer can be worth thousands of dollars. We will tell you honestly during your free review if paid search or another channel makes more sense for where you are right now.
