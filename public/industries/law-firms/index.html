Marketing that stays inside Rule 36

Law firm marketing in Australia has rules most agencies never read. Rule 36 of the Australian Solicitors' Conduct Rules requires that any advertising for a solicitor or law practice is not false, misleading or deceptive, offensive or prohibited by law. Rule 36.2 stops a firm from implying specialist expertise or using the words "accredited specialist" unless the solicitor holds that accreditation.

The Australian Consumer Law applies to your marketing as well, and responsibility for what is published stays with your firm, whoever wrote it. So we write SEO content that wins searches without overstating results, and nothing goes live until your principal has approved it.

How we keep your content compliant

  • No guaranteed outcomes or inflated success claims
  • "Specialist" used only where accreditation is held
  • Clear, accurate fee and "no win no fee" wording
  • State rules checked for personal injury pages
  • Principal sign-off before anything is published
