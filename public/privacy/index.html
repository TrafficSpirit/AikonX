How we collect, use and protect personal information.

AIkonX ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through aikonx.com.au.

Information you give us, such as your name, work email, company and message when you contact us, and basic usage data such as pages visited and device information.

How we use it

To respond to enquiries, provide and improve our services and website, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and security

We share information only with service providers who help us operate our business, or where required by law. We take reasonable steps to protect information from misuse and unauthorised access.

Your rights

You may request access to or correction of your personal information, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@aikonx.com.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies, including analytics tools, to understand how the website is used and to improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law.

Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers may store or process information outside Australia. We take reasonable steps to ensure such providers protect it appropriately.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your information, contact us at hello@aikonx.com or visit our contact page. We will respond within a reasonable time.
