Who we are

AIkonX is a digital marketing agency. We plan, launch and optimize paid search, paid social, SEO and content, and analytics and conversion programs that turn ad spend into qualified leads and predictable revenue growth.

Every account is run by senior specialists who work in the numbers. We provide live dashboards and plain-English reporting, and you always own your ad accounts, analytics and creative.

Based in Sydney, Australia. Get in touch to talk about your goals.

We start with an audit of your tracking, accounts and spend, then build a strategy tied to revenue and pipeline rather than vanity metrics. Campaigns launch across the channels that fit your goals, and we test creative, audiences and landing pages continuously.

Each month you receive a plain-English review covering revenue, pipeline and cost per acquisition, with clear next steps.
